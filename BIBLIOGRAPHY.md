# Bibliography

This bibliography lists the primary references for algorithms and techniques used in `oversampleqa`.

## Distance Metrics

- **Hassanat Distance** – A. B. Hassanat, "Dimensionality Invariant Similarity Measure," *Journal of American Science*, 10(8), 2014.
  - A. B. Hassanat et al., "On enhancing the performance of nearest neighbour classifiers using Hassanat distance metric," arXiv:1501.00687, 2015.
  - A. B. Hassanat et al., "Applications review of Hassanat distance metric," IEEE, 2022.
- **Hellinger Distance** – E. Hellinger, "Neue Begründung der Theorie quadratischer Formen von unendlichvielen Veränderlichen," 1909.
- **Jensen–Shannon Distance** – J. Lin, "Divergence Measures Based on the Shannon Entropy," 1991.
- **Minkowski and Manhattan Distances** – H. Minkowski, "Geometrie der Zahlen," 1896.
- **Euclidean Distance** – Standard metric commonly attributed to Euclid.
- **Chebyshev Distance** – Named after P. L. Chebyshev.
- **Mahalanobis Distance** – P. C. Mahalanobis, "On the Generalised Distance in Statistics," 1936.
- **Canberra Distance** – G. N. Lance and W. T. Williams, "A General Theory of Classificatory Sorting Strategies," 1966.
- **Hamming Distance** – R. W. Hamming, "Error Detecting and Error Correcting Codes," 1950.
- **Jaccard Distance** – P. Jaccard, "Étude comparative de la distribution florale," 1901.
- **Bray–Curtis Distance** – J. R. Bray and J. T. Curtis, "An ordination of the upland forest communities of southern Wisconsin," 1957.
- **Correlation Distance** – K. Pearson, "Note on Regression and Inheritance in the Case of Two Parents," 1895.
- **Energy Distance** – G. J. Székely and M. L. Rizzo, "Energy Statistics: A Class of Statistics Based on Distances," 2004.
- **Wasserstein Distance** – C. Villani, "Optimal Transport: Old and New," 2008.

## Oversampling Algorithms

- **SMOTE** – N. V. Chawla, K. W. Bowyer, L. O. Hall, and W. P. Kegelmeyer, "SMOTE: Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique," 2002.
- **ADASYN** – H. He, Y. Bai, E. A. Garcia, and S. Li, "ADASYN: Adaptive Synthetic Sampling Approach for Imbalanced Learning," 2008.

## Clustering and Dimensionality Reduction

- **k-means** – S. Lloyd, "Least Squares Quantization in PCM," 1982.
- **DBSCAN** – M. Ester, H.-P. Kriegel, J. Sander, and X. Xu, "A Density-Based Algorithm for Discovering Clusters in Large Spatial Databases with Noise," 1996.
- **UMAP** – L. McInnes, J. Healy, and J. Melville, "UMAP: Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection for Dimension Reduction," 2018.

## Validation Methodology

- **Hidden Majority Validation** – A. B. Hassanat et al., "Stop Oversampling for Class Imbalance Learning: A Critical Review," 2022.

## Statistical Inference

- **Nearest-Neighbour Two-Sample Test** – M. F. Schilling, "Multivariate Two-Sample Tests Based on Nearest Neighbors," *JASA*, 81(395), 1986.
  - N. Henze, "A Multivariate Two-Sample Test Based on the Number of Nearest Neighbor Type Coincidences," *Annals of Statistics*, 16(2), 1988.
- **Minimum Spanning Tree Test** – J. H. Friedman and L. C. Rafsky, "Multivariate Generalizations of the Wald–Wolfowitz and Smirnov Two-Sample Tests," *Annals of Statistics*, 7(4), 1979.
- **Cross-Match Test** – P. R. Rosenbaum, "An Exact Distribution-Free Test Comparing Two Multivariate Distributions Based on Adjacency," *JRSS-B*, 67(4), 2005.
- **Comparing Methods Across Datasets** – J. Demšar, "Statistical Comparisons of Classifiers over Multiple Data Sets," *JMLR*, 7, 2006.
- **False Discovery Rate** – Y. Benjamini and Y. Hochberg, "Controlling the False Discovery Rate," *JRSS-B*, 57(1), 1995.

## Fairness and Noise Diagnostics

- **Equality of Opportunity** – M. Hardt, E. Price, and N. Srebro, "Equality of Opportunity in Supervised Learning," 2016.
- **Label Noise Sensitivity** – B. Frénay and M. Verleysen, "Classification in the Presence of Label Noise: A Survey," 2014.
